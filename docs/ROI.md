# ROI

## Abandoned code is hidden capital

Most organizations and developers do not have a shortage of ideas.

They have a shortage of time to revisit dead prototypes, old internal tools, unfinished hackathon projects, and dormant experiments.

The value trapped in those repos is usually not obvious until someone spends time auditing them.

Museum of Dead Dreams exists to compress that discovery phase.

## Important note

This document uses illustrative assumptions only.

These are not audited financial claims, not customer data, and not guaranteed outcomes.

Actual value depends on:

- repo complexity
- code quality
- market timing
- team skill
- whether the underlying idea is still worth pursuing

## Illustrative assumptions

- manual repo audit: 30-60 minutes per repo
- technical revival brief: 2-4 hours per project
- internal engineering cost: $75-$150 per hour
- first-pass AI museum generation and revival framing: minutes

## Scenario table

| Scenario | Manual effort | With Museum of Dead Dreams | Estimated strategic value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Solo developer with 10 abandoned repos | 5-10 hours of audit and triage | 1 guided session plus targeted follow-up | Faster clarity on which ideas deserve a second life |
| Startup with 50 prototype repos | 25-50 hours of discovery work | Ranked backlog with generated diagnoses | Better prioritization before reinvesting engineering time |
| Hackathon team portfolio review | Re-read old code and demo context manually | Museum-style narrative + revival plans | Stronger storytelling for demos, recruiting, and follow-up builds |
| Open-source maintainer triage | Manual README and issue archaeology | Structured repo autopsy and suggested next steps | Higher signal on what is worth rescuing or archiving |
| Engineering manager evaluating internal tools | Ad hoc repo review across teams | Standardized first-pass diagnosis | Better visibility into hidden technical and product capital |

## Non-financial value

The real upside is not only time savings.

Museum of Dead Dreams can also improve:

- learning recovery
- portfolio clarity
- technical prioritization
- startup prototype evaluation
- storytelling for demos, investors, or team retrospectives

## Strategic interpretation

The product is best understood as a compression tool for:

- discovery
- diagnosis
- prioritization
- communication

It does not replace rebuilding work.
It makes the decision to rebuild or abandon much faster and more legible.
